LANDSCAPING & SITE EXECUTION

Landscape Execution That Brings the Site Together

At 7 Petals, landscaping goes beyond planting and visual design. We undertake landscape execution and allied site development works, combining hardscape, civil finishing, softscape, plantation, fabrication and irrigation wherever required.

Our execution approach focuses on creating outdoor spaces that are functional, durable, practical and visually balanced, from groundwork to finishing, coordinating multiple elements to deliver a complete landscape solution.

Our landscape execution includes

  • Hardscape works
  • Civil finishing
  • Paving & pathways
  • Outdoor structures and allied fabrication
  • Softscape & plantation
  • Lawn development
  • Garden development
  • Irrigation integration
  • Plant supply
  • Landscape maintenance support

Why professional landscape development matters

Professional landscaping creates healthier environments, improves air quality, reduces heat and adds value to a property. Green spaces support wellbeing, create inviting outdoor environments and strike a balance between nature and architecture.
