FABRICATION & ALLIED WORKS

Outdoor Structures, Fabricated and Finished

Landscapes often need more than paving and planting. 7 Petals undertakes outdoor structures and allied fabrication as part of complete landscape projects, so structures, finishes and planting are coordinated by one team.

Our hardscape project files include pergolas with electrical works, MS fencing around play courts, steel railings, trellis, LED lighting and water bodies built in RCC with fabrication, fountain and electrical works.

Allied works we execute

  • Pergolas & shelters
  • MS fencing & railings
  • Trellis & lattice screens
  • LED & landscape lighting
  • Electrical works for outdoor features
  • RCC water bodies with fountains
  • Outdoor seating structures
  • Play-court fencing and surfacing
