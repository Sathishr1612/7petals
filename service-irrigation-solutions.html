IRRIGATION SOLUTIONS

Efficient Watering for Healthy Landscapes

An effective irrigation system is essential for maintaining healthy landscapes while using water efficiently. 7 Petals provides irrigation solutions for gardens, plantations and landscaped areas, including drip irrigation and sprinkler irrigation.

Every system is planned around the landscape layout, plant types, soil and water availability, so water reaches where it is needed with less waste.

SYSTEMS

Types of
Irrigation We Install

Drip irrigation

Delivers water to the root zone of plants, minimising wastage. Suited to shrubs, flower beds, plantations and terrace gardens.

Sprinkler irrigation

Distributes water evenly across lawns, gardens and larger landscapes.

Automatic irrigation

Programmable controllers and timers regulate schedules so plants are watered consistently.

BENEFITS

Benefits of a
professional irrigation system

Water conservation

Water is delivered only where it is required.

Healthier plants

Consistent watering supports stronger roots, healthier lawns and flowering.

Lower maintenance effort

Automation reduces manual watering and labour.

Better coverage

Every part of the landscape gets adequate moisture without dry patches or overwatering.
