Does 7 Petals undertake hardscape work?

Does 7 Petals provide softscape services?

Yes. Softscape services include lawn development, plantation, trees, shrubs, garden development, soil preparation and other greenery-related works.

Does 7 Petals only provide landscape design?

No. Our focus is landscape execution and outdoor works, with particular emphasis on hardscape, civil finishing and softscape implementation.

What is the difference between hardscape and softscape?

Hardscape refers to the built elements of a landscape, such as paving, pathways, outdoor flooring, edging, steps and other structural or civil finishing elements. Softscape refers to living and natural elements such as lawns, trees, plants, shrubs and garden areas.

Can hardscape and softscape works be executed together?

Yes. Combining hardscape and softscape helps create outdoor spaces where functional built elements and greenery work together as a complete landscape.
