About 7 Petals.
Creating Green Spaces.

At 7 Petals Horticulture (India) Pvt. Ltd, we specialize in creating green spaces that blend beauty, sustainability, and functionality. Established in 2014, our leadership team brings over 25+ years of landscaping and horticulture expertise, delivering customized solutions for residential, commercial, industrial, and institutional projects.

From landscape design and terrace gardens to tree transplantation, irrigation systems, vertical gardens, and annual maintenance, we transform every outdoor space into a thriving environment. We believe every project deserves creativity, quality workmanship, and long-lasting value.

What landscaping services do you provide?

We offer landscape design, hardscape, softscape, terrace gardens, vertical gardens, irrigation systems, tree transplantation, plant supply, garden maintenance, and horticulture consultancy.

Do you work on residential and commercial projects?

Yes. We undertake residential, commercial, industrial, institutional, and corporate landscaping projects.

Do you provide annual garden maintenance?

Yes. We offer customized annual maintenance contracts to keep landscapes healthy throughout the year.

Absolutely. We design and install efficient irrigation systems tailored to your landscape requirements.
