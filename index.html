Frequently Asked Questions

What is the typical timeline for a custom villa landscape project?

A luxury villa landscape project typically takes 3 to 6 weeks from initial 3D design approval to complete site execution, depending on site size, soil grading, and custom water features.

Can you install living vertical gardens in penthouse apartments?

Yes, our vertical garden systems use lightweight modular hydroponic felt matrices and automated drip lines, making them perfectly safe and clean for penthouse balconies and living spaces.

Do you provide ongoing post-completion maintenance services?

Absolutely. We offer annual horticulture maintenance retainer programs where dedicated gardeners and arborists visit weekly to prune, nourish, and check automated irrigation lines.
