7 Petals Horticulture (India) Pvt. Ltd. (“7 Petals”, “we”) respects your privacy. This page explains how information is handled when you use this website.

Information you give us

When you contact us by phone, email, WhatsApp or the enquiry form, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, email address, project location and message. The enquiry form on this website does not store anything on the site; it prepares a message in your own email app or WhatsApp, which you then send.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange consultations or quotations.
  • To deliver and follow up on services you request.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We may share it with project partners or service providers only where needed to deliver the work you request, or where required by law.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently set advertising cookies. If analytics or similar tools are added, this policy will be updated.

Third-party links

Links such as WhatsApp open services run by third parties, with their own privacy practices.

Your choices
