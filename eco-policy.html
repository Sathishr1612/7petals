7 Petals Horticulture works with plants, soil and water every day, so responsible environmental practice is part of our work. This draft summarises commitments drawn from our approach; it is to be confirmed by the company.

Our approach

  • Efficient water use: drip and sprinkler irrigation designed to reduce water wastage.
  • Right plant, right place: species selected for climate, soil and maintenance needs to support healthy, long-lived planting.
  • Preserving mature trees: tree transplantation as an alternative to removal where site conditions and tree health permit.
  • Durable construction: well-executed hardscape and finishing that lasts, reducing rework and waste.
  • Own nursery: plants grown and sourced through our nursery at Karjat.
