PLANT NURSERY & PLANT SUPPLY

Plants for Every Landscape

Healthy plants are the foundation of a successful green space. 7 Petals’ plant nursery and plant supply services support landscaping, gardening, plantation and horticulture requirements with a range of plants suited to different applications.

We are supported by a nursery at Karjat, where plants for our landscapes are grown as well as sourced and stored. Our horticulturists help clients choose the right species for sunlight, soil, water and climate.
